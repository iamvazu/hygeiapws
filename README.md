# Hygeia Power Wash Solutions — Official Web & Application Platform

Modern, full-featured commercial pressure washing, residential power washing, and monthly curbside trash bin sanitizing web application suite engineered for Silicon Valley and San Francisco Peninsula communities.

## Overview & Capabilities
- **50 Production-Ready HTML Pages**: Comprehensive coverage for all services, 14 Silicon Valley & Peninsula cities, and 21 keyword-dense SEO/AEO/GEO blog articles.
- **Client Subscription Portal (`portal.html`)**: Dynamic bin count adjuster, monthly vs. one-time plan switcher, 256-bit SSL simulated Stripe card checkout, municipal trash collection countdown, and before/after proof viewer.
- **Admin Operations Center (`admin.html`)**: Executive KPI dashboard, route stop status optimizer, tech simulator, and commercial quote builder.
- **Interactive Silicon Valley Animated Map**: Real California/Bay Area vector coastline with live animated wash truck cruising across all 14 service areas.
- **4-Step "How It Works" Section**: Complete with animated Hygeia mobile wash rig illustration and thermal specification pills.
- **State of California Compliance**: DVBE Certified, Small Business (Micro) Certified (#2054658), and DIR Public Works Registered (#JS-LR-100 132 4606) with official CA.GOV, DGS, and FI$Cal certification badges.
- **AI & Answer Engine Optimization**: Complete `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, XML sitemap (`sitemap.xml`), and Schema.org JSON-LD microdata.

## Local Development
To serve locally:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080 --directory website
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.
