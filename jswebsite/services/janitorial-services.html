Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Janitorial Operations FAQs

Straight answers regarding keys, security, insurance, staffing, and compliance.

How does Hygeia manage security clearance, building keycards, and alarm codes?

Every Hygeia janitorial team member undergoes thorough background verification, drug screening, and confidentiality training before receiving site access. Keys and access fobs are held in locked key boxes, and alarm arming/disarming is digitally logged with time-stamped confirmation sent to building operations.

Can we combine daytime porter services with overnight janitorial crews?

Yes. Many Silicon Valley tech campuses and high-density office towers operate a hybrid model: dedicated Day Porters manage continual restroom checks, coffee station maintenance, and spill emergencies during peak business hours, while our Night Janitorial crew handles heavy deep cleaning, vacuuming, and trash consolidation after staff departs.

What certifications does Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia carry for public tenders?

Hygeia is an official State of California Certified Small Business (Micro) and Certified Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise (DVBE #2054658), fully registered in FI$Cal with Department of Industrial Relations (DIR #JS-LR-100 132 4606) public works compliance. We help primes and municipal agencies satisfy state diversity mandates.

What if we need immediate emergency biohazard or water cleanup?

Our Santa Clara operations dispatch center operates 24/7/365. Contracted facilities enjoy priority 60-to-90 minute emergency response for restroom overflows, flood extraction, unexpected pathogen containment, or pre-executive inspection walk preparations.
