1. Agreement to Terms & Contracting Parties

These Terms of Service ("Terms", "Agreement") constitute a legally binding contractual agreement between Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia Commercial Cleaning & Janitorial Services ("Hygeia", "Contractor", "we", "us", or "our"), a California corporation headquartered at 1105 Benton St, Suite A, Santa Clara, CA 95050, holding State of California Certified Small Business and Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise (DVBE) Certification ID #2054658 and California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) Public Works Registration #JS-LR-100 132 4606, and the individual, commercial entity, corporate tenant, property management company, healthcare institution, or institutional facility owner ("Client", "Customer", "Facility Manager", or "you") executing a Proposal, Work Order, Service Agreement, or digital service intake via our website or Client Portal.

By executing a Service Agreement, accepting an operational proposal, scheduling service via our Client Portal, or permitting Hygeia personnel to access the designated commercial property, you agree to comply with and be bound by all terms, conditions, and operational policies set forth herein.

2. Commercial Service Scope & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

Hygeia delivers customized commercial sanitization, custodial services, and specialized facility maintenance according to the specific Statement of Work (SOW) agreed upon in the Client's Master Agreement or verified Work Order. Services may incorporate:

  • Scheduled Nightly & Evening Janitorial: Restroom sanitization and restocking, touchpoint disinfection, breakroom/kitchenette detailing, trash and recycling disposal, high-filtration HEPA vacuuming, and hard-surface floor dust-mopping and damp-mopping.
  • Dedicated Day Porter Staffing: Continuous daytime facility upkeep, continuous executive restroom inspections, spill response, conference center resets, lobby glass polishing, outdoor perimeter trash sweeps, and coffee station maintenance.
  • Medical Suite & Healthcare Terminal Cleaning: Operating room and exam room terminal disinfection adhering strictly to CDC, CMS, AAAHC, and OSHA standards utilizing EPA-registered List N hospital-grade virucides with verified contact kill times.
  • Biotech & ISO Cleanroom Protocol Maintenance: Controlled-environment decontamination (ISO Class 7 and Class 8) using non-shedding micro-denier wipes, lint-free specialized mop systems, and cleanroom-approved quaternary and sporicidal agents.
  • Specialty Hard Floor Care & Carpet Remediation: Periodic high-speed machine burnishing, rotary scrubbing, top-coating, chemical stripping and refinishing (waxing), and deep hot-water extraction commercial carpet steam cleaning.
  • Emergency Response & Work Orders: Rapid 24/7 dispatch for fluid spills, plumbing overflows, post-event cleanups, or unscheduled decontamination requested via the Hygeia Client Portal or 24/7 Operations Line.

All work is executed in accordance with documented Hygeia standard operating procedures (SOPs). Any additions or modifications to the scope of work requested outside the active contract must be memorialized through a supplemental digital Work Order generated through the Client Portal or confirmed in writing by authorized Hygeia operations management.

3. Facility Access, Keycards, Security & Alarm Protocol

To facilitate efficient execution of night shifts and weekend cleanings, the Client must grant authorized Hygeia personnel safe and timely access to the subject premises:

  • Security Credentials & Key Control: Client shall provide necessary physical keys, electronic FOBs, proximity cards, and dock access codes. Keys and electronic credentials are coded anonymously and held in dual-custody lockboxes. Hygeia strictly forbids the unauthorized copying, transfer, or off-site removal of client keys.
  • Alarm Systems & Disarming Procedures: Client shall provide written instructions, arming/disarming sequences, and dedicated security codes for commercial intrusion alarms. Hygeia logs all building arrivals and departures digitally. If an alarm activates due to inaccurate codes provided by the Client or failure of Client alarm hardware, Hygeia shall not be liable for municipal false alarm fines.
  • Personnel Verification & Background Screening: Every Hygeia cleaning specialist, shift supervisor, and day porter is legally authorized to work in the United States, fully bonded, and passes a comprehensive multi-jurisdictional background check (including criminal history, identity verification, and pre-placement drug screening) prior to assignment.
  • Locked-Out / Access Interruption Charge: If a scheduled Hygeia crew is dispatched to a facility but denied entry due to changed security codes, locked gates, malfunctioning keycard readers, or client-initiated security lockouts without at least four (4) hours prior notice, a Trip Minimum Charge equal to 50% of the scheduled shift value (or a minimum of $150.00) will be assessed to offset mobilized labor.

4. Client Facility Obligations & Utilities Access

The Client shall be responsible for ensuring the following operational conditions during all scheduled service windows:

  • Utilities: Continuous access to functioning electricity, running cold and hot water, adequate interior lighting, and operational janitorial closet drainage.
  • Storage Facilities: Where agreed upon for recurring contracts, Client will furnish a locked, dedicated janitor closet or secure storage area for Hygeia equipment, clean microfiber supplies, and HEPA vacuums.
  • Hazard Disclosure: Client must proactively inform Hygeia management of any known hazardous conditions, biohazard risks, broken glass, uncontained chemical reagents, exposed wiring, asbestos-containing materials, or active pest infestations on the premises prior to crew entry.

5. Commercial Rates, Invoicing, Billing & Retainers

All rates, payment frequencies, and billing conditions are established in the Client’s executed Proposal or Service Agreement:

  • Recurring Retainer Invoicing: Monthly janitorial and day porter retainers are billed in advance on the 1st of each service month or on agreed Net 15 / Net 30 billing cycles. Billing invoices are transmitted electronically and available in real time within the Hygeia Client Portal.
  • Approved Payment Methods: Hygeia accepts Automated Clearing House (ACH) direct corporate debits, commercial bank wires, corporate checks mailed to our corporate office, and major corporate credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
  • Late Fees & Delinquencies: Invoices outstanding past the thirty (30) day due date shall accrue a late interest penalty of 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum allowable rate under California law, whichever is lower. Hygeia reserves the right to suspend scheduled cleaning operations for accounts more than forty-five (45) days delinquent until past-due balances are satisfied in full.
  • Supply Pass-Through Costs: Unless expressly structured as an "all-inclusive" contract, consumable restocking supplies (e.g., multifold paper towels, roll toilet tissue, foam hand soap cartridges, toilet seat covers, heavy-duty trash can liners) furnished by Hygeia will be billed as a line-item pass-through cost based on actual monthly consumption.

6. Term, Scheduling, Rescheduling & Contract Cancellation

  • Agreement Duration: Commercial cleaning service agreements typically operate on a continuous month-to-month, one-year, or multi-year basis as stipulated in the contract document.
  • Voluntary Cancellation (Master Agreements): Either party may terminate a recurring commercial janitorial agreement for convenience by providing thirty (30) days advance written notice to the other party via certified mail or via electronic confirmation to aloha@hygeiaservices.com. This thirty-day notice period ensures fair transition planning and ethical labor reallocation for assigned personnel.
  • Immediate Termination for Cause: Either party may immediately terminate an active agreement if the other party commits a material breach (such as persistent non-payment, compromise of facility security, or refusal to correct documented safety violations) that remains uncured for seven (7) business days following written demand.
  • Rescheduling Single Service Visits: Specialty floor care, cleanroom terminal scrubs, or extra dispatches must be rescheduled with at least twenty-four (24) hours advance notice. Cancellations made inside twenty-four hours are subject to a late-cancellation fee of $100.00.

7. Performance Guarantee & 24-Hour Re-Clean Warranty

Hygeia maintains a 100% Client Satisfaction Guarantee across all serviced commercial square footage. If any aspect of our cleaning fails to satisfy the agreed-upon Statement of Work or passes below facility quality standards:

  • The Client must report the deficient area within twenty-four (24) hours of shift completion through the Hygeia Client Portal, or by telephoning dispatch at (650) 933-3823.
  • Upon verification, Hygeia will immediately dispatch a supervisor or field specialist to re-clean the affected area at zero additional charge within twelve (12) hours or prior to the start of the next business day.
  • Timely notice is required to enable our quality assurance team to inspect conditions before normal daily office foot traffic degrades the area. Deficiency reports submitted beyond 24 hours will be addressed on the next regularly scheduled shift.

8. Insurance Coverage, Bonding & Mutual Limitation of Liability

Hygeia maintains robust, institutional-grade commercial insurance to protect client assets and property under management:

  • Commercial General Liability: $5,000,000 General Aggregate / $2,000,000 Per Occurrence coverage against third-party bodily injury and property damage.
  • Workers' Compensation: Comprehensive statutory California Workers’ Compensation coverage covering 100% of field operatives, supervisors, and administrative personnel.
  • Janitorial Surety Bond: Blanket commercial fidelity bond covering employee dishonesty and property protection.
  • Certificates of Insurance (COI): Prior to commencement of service, Hygeia will furnish a standard ACORD Certificate of Insurance designating the Client and building owner/property management firm as Additional Insured with appropriate primary/non-contributory endorsements.
  • Mutual Limitation of Liability: To the fullest extent permitted by California law, neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages (including loss of business profits or data interruption) arising out of or related to this Agreement. Hygeia’s total aggregate liability for direct damages arising under an agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client during the three (3) months immediately preceding the incident giving rise to the claim.

9. Environmental Health, CDC List N & Bloodborne Pathogens Protocol

Hygeia operates in strict compliance with California Division of Occupational Safety and Health (Cal/OSHA) mandates and environmental stewardship guidelines:

  • Green Cleaning & Indoor Air Quality: We utilize Green Seal (GS-37) certified, zero-VOC cleaning agents, color-coded lint-free microfiber textiles to eradicate cross-contamination, and multi-stage CRI-certified HEPA filtration vacuums that capture 99.97% of airborne particles down to 0.3 microns.
  • Medical & Biological Standards: Disinfectants employed in clinical, healthcare, and high-touch commercial zones are registered on the EPA List N: Disinfectants for Coronavirus & Emerging Viral Pathogens. Cleaners maintain verified label contact dwell times for maximum microbial eradication.
  • Bloodborne Pathogens & Biohazards: Our operations personnel undergo mandatory annual training under the OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030). Clients must notify management immediately if blood, bodily fluids, or biological wastes are present. Specialized PPE, containment bags, and regulated waste manifest disposal procedures will be initiated.

10. Non-Solicitation of Janitorial Personnel & Supervisors

Hygeia invests substantial time, financial capital, and professional development in recruiting, vetting, background-checking, and training its custodial personnel, day porters, and field managers. In recognition of this investment:

The Client agrees that during the term of any active service agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following contract termination, the Client shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, hire, contract with, or employ any current or former Hygeia cleaning specialist, shift supervisor, or operations manager who provided services at the Client’s facility, unless prior written consent has been granted by an officer of Montero Enterprises Inc.

In the event of a breach of this section, Client agrees to pay Hygeia as agreed liquidated damages (and not as a penalty) an amount equal to thirty percent (30%) of the hired employee's annualized wages, reflecting the reasonable replacement recruitment, onboarding, and training costs incurred by Hygeia.

11. Force Majeure & Operational Emergencies

Neither party shall be held liable for failure or delay in performing contractual obligations if such failure arises from circumstances beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to: acts of God, earthquakes, wildfires, severe flooding, national or regional power grid failures, public utility interruptions, civil unrest, declared public health states of emergency, mandatory civic lockdowns, or regional transit closures. In such events, both parties will cooperate in good faith to resume commercial operations as soon as safe facility access is restored.

Any legal action, dispute, or proceeding arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state courts located in Santa Clara County, California, or the United States District Court for the Northern District of California (San Jose Division). The prevailing party in any action to enforce the terms of this Agreement shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney’s fees, court costs, and related legal expenses.
