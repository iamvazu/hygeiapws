Code § 1798.100 et seq.) and corresponding regulations promulgated by the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA).

5. Non-Sale & Non-Sharing Pledge

Under the CCPA, "sale" is defined broadly to include selling, renting, releasing, disclosing, disseminating, making available, transferring, or communicating personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration. "Sharing" refers to disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Official CCPA Affirmative Statement:
• Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia does NOT sell personal information.
• We have NOT sold any personal information in the preceding twelve (12) months.
• We do NOT share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
• We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing personal information of consumers under sixteen (16) years of age.

6. Sensitive Personal Information (SPI)

Hygeia collects Client Portal passwords solely in salted, cryptographically hashed form (PBKDF2 SHA-512) for account authentication. We do not collect or process sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, racial background, religious beliefs, genetic data, or biometric data) for the purpose of inferring characteristics about consumers. Therefore, the Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information under Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.121 is satisfied by our operational restraint.

7. Your Statutory California Consumer Rights

If you are a California resident, you possess the following protected legal rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • 1. Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request that we disclose the categories of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the business purposes for collecting it, the categories of third parties with whom we disclose it, and the specific pieces of personal information collected about you over the preceding twelve (12) months.
  • 2. Right to Delete: You have the right to request that we delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to statutory exceptions (such as completing an ongoing contract, maintaining required tax records, detecting security fraud, or complying with legal obligations).
  • 3. Right to Correct Inaccurate Personal Information: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate personal information maintained in your active customer record.
  • 4. Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: Because Hygeia does not sell or share personal information, our operations inherently honor this requirement.
  • 5. Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. Unless permitted by law, we will not deny you services, charge you different rates or prices, or provide a differentiated level or quality of janitorial care.

8. Submitting a Verifiable California Consumer Request

To exercise your California Right to Know, Access, Correct, or Delete, please submit a verifiable consumer request through one of the following official channels:

  • Toll-Free Telephone: Call our administrative office at (650) 933-3823 and state that you are making a "California CCPA Consumer Request."
  • Direct Certified Email: Email our legal division at aloha@hygeiaservices.com with the subject line "CCPA / CPRA Request for California Resident".
  • Postal Mail: Write to Montero Enterprises Inc., Attn: California Privacy Officer, 1105 Benton St, Suite A, Santa Clara, CA 95050.

9. Identity Verification Standards

To safeguard your facility records and prevent unauthorized disclosure, we must verify your identity before processing any Request to Know, Correct, or Delete. Our verification protocol requires:

  • Two-Point Verification: Matching at least two data points provided in your request (such as your full legal name, corporate email address, and facility telephone number) against verified records in our secure database.
  • Three-Point Verification for Sensitive Records: For requests seeking specific pieces of personal data, we require matching at least three verified data points plus a signed declaration under penalty of perjury confirming your identity.
  • We will confirm receipt of your request within ten (10) business days and provide substantive response within forty-five (45) calendar days, as permitted by California statute.

10. Authorized Agent Representation

You may designate an authorized legal agent to submit a request on your behalf. To designate an authorized agent, you must provide the agent with written, signed authorization, and the agent must verify their own identity with us or provide proof of valid power of attorney pursuant to California Probate Code §§ 4121 to 4130.

11. California "Shine the Light" Law Disclosures

California Civil Code § 1798.83 ("Shine the Light" law) permits California residents to request certain information regarding our disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. Hygeia does not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.
