1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage Technologies?

Cookies are small text data files placed on your computer, mobile device, or tablet by websites that you visit. They are widely utilized by modern web platforms to make websites work efficiently, provide enhanced functionality, preserve session security, and furnish aggregate diagnostic data to website operators.

In addition to traditional HTTP cookies, modern web browsers utilize comparable data storage technologies, such as HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage. Throughout this Policy, we use the term "cookies" collectively to describe browser cookies, session tokens, local storage objects, and related web technologies.

2. Why Hygeia Uses Cookies

Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia uses cookies strictly to deliver a smooth, responsive, and secure digital experience for commercial facility managers, enterprise procurement leads, and portal clients. Specifically, cookies enable us to:

  • Secure Client Portal Authentication: Maintain your verified login state when navigating between portal dashboards, reviewing nightly cleaning schedules, and approving facility work orders.
  • Interactive Estimator Persistence: Retain inputs entered into our Janitorial Price Estimator (such as facility square footage, cleaning frequency, and building sector) so you do not have to re-enter parameters during your visit.
  • Platform Protection & CSRF Defense: Defend forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks and malicious automated bot spam.
  • Server Performance & Uptime Telemetry: Assess server response speeds, API latency, and content delivery network (CDN) routing to guarantee rapid page rendering.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

Our website and client applications implement the following defined classifications of cookies:

A. Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies

These cookies are indispensable for the operation of our website and client portal. Without these cookies, services you have expressly requested (such as authenticating your client account, submitting an RFP request, or accessing confidential facility work orders) cannot function. Because these cookies are required for platform security and operation, they cannot be switched off in our systems.

B. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make while interacting with our site (such as your preferred enterprise division view in admin consoles, or square footage selections in interactive calculators). Their purpose is to provide an optimized, personalized user experience.

C. Performance & Web Analytics Telemetry (Google Analytics)

These cookies collect aggregated, pseudonymous information regarding website traffic volume, visitor navigation pathways, session durations, and user interactions with our service calculators. We use Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Search Console to understand which pages (such as commercial cleaning services, day porter options, and facility sector pages) are most useful to commercial property managers, measure page loading speeds, and detect server-side errors.

Google Analytics operates with IP masking/anonymization enabled to protect individual visitor identities. We do not use Google Analytics to identify specific visitors or merge analytics data with personally identifiable customer information.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising Pixels Policy

Unlike consumer media sites, Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia is a dedicated B2B commercial contractor. We do not embed third-party advertising trackers, cross-site behavioral retargeting pixels (such as Meta/Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or Google Display Remarketing), or data broker tracking scripts on our pages.

Your activity on our website is never shared with external programmatic ad networks to serve you advertisements across other internet destinations.

6. Browser Management & Opt-Out Options

You have full authority to control, limit, or purge cookies via your web browser’s privacy settings. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies and local storage tokens are stored on your device.
  • Block third-party cookies or cookies from specific websites.
  • Configure your browser to automatically clear all cookies upon exiting.
  • Block all cookies unconditionally.

To adjust cookie settings in your preferred browser, follow the official instructions provided by your browser manufacturer:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari (macOS & iOS): Preferences / Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Opting Out of Google Analytics: In addition to browser-level cookie controls, you may specifically opt out of having your page visits, dwell time, and interaction pathways recorded by Google Analytics by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Important Operational Note: If you elect to block or disable all cookies in your browser, the public marketing pages of our website will continue to display normally. However, you will be unable to log into the Hygeia Client Portal or access live work orders, as encrypted session tokens are necessary for authorized identity verification.

7. Global Privacy Control (GPC) & Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Hygeia respects automated privacy signals. When our web servers detect a valid Global Privacy Control (GPC) or "Do Not Track" (DNT) header emitted by your browser, our systems automatically refrain from deploying any non-essential analytics and enforce our strict non-sharing protocol.
