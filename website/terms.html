2. Curbside Bin Cleaning Subscriptions & Billing

Our bin cleaning subscription services operate on an automated recurring basis:

  • Monthly Clean Plan ($35/month): Includes 2 standard municipal bins washed every 4 weeks. Additional bins are billed at $10.00 each per month.
