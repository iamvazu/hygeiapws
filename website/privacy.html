Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and respect your personal information.

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 Compliance: CCPA / CPRA & CalOPPA Entity: Hygeia Power Wash Solutions
Key Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to schedule exterior washing, provide quotes, and manage your curbside service.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request an estimate, or subscribe to bin cleaning services, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: First and last name, email address, and phone number.
  • Service Property Information: Service street address, city, zip code, and gate/access codes provided for route scheduling.
  • Project Uploads: Photos or architectural files you voluntarily upload to assist our estimating team.
  • Billing Information: Payment card details processed securely via tokenized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors. We do not store raw card numbers on our local servers.
  • Technical Usage: IP address, device type, browser information, and referral sources collected via essential cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Generating accurate commercial and residential project estimates.
  • Dispatching service vehicles and route technicians to your address.
  • Processing recurring monthly subscription billing and sending digital receipts.
  • Communicating service schedules, weather delays, or arrival notifications.
  • Ensuring compliance with local environmental and California regulatory standards.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist our operations under strict confidentiality obligations (e.g., cloud payment gateways, route optimization software, and SMS dispatch systems).

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, secure access controls, and regular security reviews. Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to maintain active service accounts or fulfill legal tax and accounting requirements.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of your account records. For California residents, please refer to our dedicated California Privacy (CCPA) Notice.
