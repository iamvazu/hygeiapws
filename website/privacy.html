1. Scope & Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy outlines the operational policies and data governance standards of Montero Enterprises Inc. dba Hygeia Power Wash Solutions ("Hygeia", "we", "us", or "our"), governing personal, commercial, and property data gathered through our primary website (hygeiapws.com), the Hygeia PWS Client Portal (portal.html), 24/7 telephone dispatch at (650) 933-3823, digital estimate submission forms, and on-site commercial property assessments conducted across our 14 dedicated service routes in the San Francisco Bay Area and Silicon Valley.

We strictly comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA), the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (CPRA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), and all applicable municipal and federal privacy regulations.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

Depending on whether you are requesting commercial power washing, residential exterior maintenance, or subscribing to curbside trash bin sanitizing, we collect the following classifications of data:

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, company or property management name, corporate title, email address, direct telephone number, and physical property address.
  • Service Property Specifications: Property type (commercial parking garage, retail center, HOA community, industrial loading dock, private residence), estimated square footage, surface substrates (concrete flatwork, stucco, paver stones, brick, architectural metal), number and color of trash/recycle/compost bins, municipal waste pickup days, and on-site water spigot availability.
  • Property Photographs & Work Scopes: Digital photos or site plans uploaded via our contact forms depicting oil stains, dumpster pads, grease buildup, or siding conditions, utilized exclusively for accurate proposal scoping and technician routing.
  • Client Portal Credentials: Account username, email address, salted PBKDF2 password hashes, assigned client identifier (e.g. HYG-PWS-1049), designated wash truck rig (e.g. Rig-01), and active service subscription plan tiers.
  • Billing & Transaction Details: Invoicing records, subscription renewal schedules, work order authorizations, and payment receipts. All electronic credit card and ACH transactions are securely processed through encrypted PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant gateways. Hygeia does not store raw credit card numbers or bank PINs on its web servers.
  • Automated Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, operating system, timestamps, referring search engines, and aggregated usage metrics collected via Google Analytics and Google Search Console.

3. Collection Touchpoints

Information is collected through three primary interactions:

  1. Direct Customer Submission: Completing our online "Get a Quote" or RFP forms, selecting a curbside bin cleaning plan in the calculator, signing up for the PWS Client Portal, telephoning dispatch, or emailing our operations team.
  2. Field Inspections & Service Execution: On-site pre-wash inspections, photographic documentation of curbside bin condition, and GPS-verified truck stop completion logs generated by our mobile wash rigs.
  3. Automated Analytics Technologies: Server HTTP transaction logs, essential functional cookies, Google Analytics tracking scripts, and Search Console telemetry.

4. Operational Business Uses

Hygeia processes collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and logistical purposes:

  • Accurate Estimating & Proposal Delivery: Evaluating exterior square footage, oil saturation, and surface materials to generate binding commercial bids.
  • Mobile Fleet Route Optimization: Clustering curbside bin sanitization stops by municipal trash collection schedules across 14 Bay Area cities, assigning stops to mobile rigs (Rig-01 / Rig-02), and minimizing vehicle emissions.
  • Job Execution & Dispatch: Dispatching crews for overnight commercial flatwork washing, parking garage degreasing, and loading dock maintenance.
  • Customer Communications & Verification: Sending route arrival notifications, invoice receipts, 24-hour satisfaction follow-ups, and emergency weather rescheduling notices.
  • Environmental Reporting: Documenting compliance with California Regional Water Quality Control Board zero-runoff regulations and municipal stormwater discharge permits.
  • State Supplier Compliance: Fulfilling California Department of General Services (DGS) Small Business / DVBE (#2054658) reporting and Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) public works payroll documentation.

5. Website Analytics: Google Analytics & Google Search Console

To evaluate how prospective commercial clients, property managers, and Bay Area residents discover and interact with our exterior cleaning services, Hygeia utilizes web analytics services provided by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), specifically Google Analytics (Google Analytics 4 / GA4) and Google Search Console.

A. What Google Analytics Collects & Tracks

Google Analytics uses cookies and device identifiers to generate aggregated, pseudonymous statistical reports regarding visitor behavior, including:

  • Page Views & User Journeys: Which individual service pages (e.g., commercial pressure washing, residential power washing, curbside bin cleaning, concrete cleaning), city route directories, and blog guides visitors view; the time spent on each page; click interactions (such as "Get a Quote" and "Request Commercial RFP"); and user navigation paths throughout the site.
  • Device & Technical Attributes: Device type (desktop, smartphone, tablet), web browser brand and version, operating system, display screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Traffic Channels & Referral Sources: How users arrived at our site, including organic search engine queries, direct URL entry, social referrals, or partner links.
  • IP Masking & Anonymization: We implement Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled. Your IP address is truncated and masked to prevent individual personal identification before being processed by Google servers.

B. Google Search Console & Search Performance

We use Google Search Console to evaluate aggregate search engine performance, monitor crawl errors, and track organic keyword queries that led users to our website (such as "commercial pressure washing San Jose" or "trash bin cleaning Santa Clara"). Search Console reports total search impressions, click counts, and average ranking positions without tracking or revealing individual personal identities.

C. Opting Out of Google Analytics

If you prefer not to have your web browsing session analyzed by Google Analytics, you may opt out by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You may also configure your browser to block analytics cookies or enable Global Privacy Control (GPC).

6. Sharing & Disclosure Limitations

We consider your property and personal data confidential. We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or share information with third-party advertisers or external data brokers. Data is disclosed strictly to the following entities solely to fulfill service operations:

  • Employed Fleet Technicians & Route Supervisors: Directly employed, bonded, and background-screened Hygeia operators who need property addresses and gate access notes to perform washing.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers, encrypted database administrators, and PCI-compliant merchant processors under strict non-disclosure terms.
  • Municipal & Regulatory Authorities: Water reclamation inspectors or regulatory agencies where required by California environmental law or court subpoena.

7. Security & Technical Encryption Standards

Hygeia enforces rigorous technical, administrative, and physical protections to safeguard your records:

  • 256-Bit TLS Encryption: All data transmitted to and from our website and Client Portal is secured using TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols.
  • Salted Cryptographic Hashing: Client Portal account passwords are encrypted using salted PBKDF2 SHA-512 key derivation functions. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Restricted Access Controls: Access to client billing information, gate codes, and property records is restricted strictly to authorized operations management.
  • Active Server Monitoring: VPS firewalls, brute-force throttles, and routine security patch updates are maintained continuously.

8. Data Retention & Secure Digital Disposal

We retain records only for as long as necessary to satisfy operational and legal requirements:

  • Active Customer Profiles: Account details, service addresses, and bin subscription schedules are maintained for the active duration of the customer relationship.
  • Tax & Accounting Records: Invoices, payment receipts, and commercial work orders are preserved for seven (7) years in compliance with California Revenue and Taxation Code and IRS regulations.
  • Unconverted Quote Inquiries: Exploratory RFP inquiries that do not result in service are archived and securely purged after twenty-four (24) months.

9. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal contact data from our active records at any time. California residents have additional statutory protections detailed in our dedicated California Consumer Privacy Notice.

10. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our website and pressure washing services are directed exclusively at property owners, commercial business managers, and adult consumers over the age of eighteen (18). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

11. Third-Party Links & Sister Divisions

Our website contains links to our sister commercial division, Hygeia Commercial Janitorial Services (hygeiaservices.com), and official state regulatory bodies (e.g., California DGS, DIR, Water Boards). Independent third-party websites operate under their own privacy policies.
