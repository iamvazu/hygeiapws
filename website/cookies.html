1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage Technologies?

Cookies are compact text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet by websites you visit. They are universally employed across modern web platforms to make sites function smoothly, safeguard user account sessions, preserve input selections, and provide anonymous operational telemetry to site owners.

In addition to standard cookies, modern web browsers employ HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage. These technologies store data locally within your browser to enable fast, offline-capable application states (such as remembering your chosen bin cleaning schedule or preserving interactive calculator totals). Throughout this Policy, the term "cookies" encompasses cookies, local storage objects, and session tokens.

2. Why Hygeia Uses Cookies

Hygeia Power Wash Solutions uses cookies exclusively to power our operational web features and provide a seamless booking experience for Bay Area homeowners, commercial property managers, and municipal coordinators:

  • Securing Client Portal Sessions: Allowing bin cleaning subscribers and commercial facility leads to log into portal.html, track their next scheduled truck rig visit, and approve extra wash dispatches without needing to re-authenticate on every page.
  • Preserving Instant Estimator Selections: Remembering your chosen trash can count (e.g. 2, 3, or 4 cans) and commercial square footage in our instant pricing tools.
  • Platform Security & Bot Defense: Shielding quote submission forms against automated script spam, brute-force intrusions, and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).
  • Evaluating Technical Performance: Monitoring page rendering speeds, mobile asset delivery, and API server uptime across our California hosting infrastructure.

3. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

Our website utilizes three discrete classifications of cookies:

A. Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies

These cookies are essential to the core functionality and security of our web properties. They enable encrypted session authentication, CSRF token validation, and secure dispatch form submissions. Because our website and customer portal cannot operate securely without them, essential cookies cannot be disabled via site preferences.

B. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies remember selections you have made during your browsing session—such as selected service frequencies (monthly vs. quarterly bin cleaning), property type (commercial vs. residential), or your view preference in admin dashboards. They ensure you do not have to reconfigure settings each time you visit.

C. Performance & Web Analytics Telemetry (Google Analytics & Search Console)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Search Console to understand how users find and use our website. These tools track which service pages (commercial power washing, dumpster pad degreasing, curbside bin sanitizing, 14 city service route pages, and blog guides) are viewed most often, how much time visitors spend reviewing our wash process, navigation flow, and click interactions on "Get a Quote" buttons.

All Google Analytics tracking is executed with IP masking/anonymization enabled. Google Analytics data is aggregated and pseudonymous; it is never matched with your name, service address, or customer account records.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising Pixels Policy

Hygeia Power Wash Solutions is a professional exterior maintenance and mobile fleet contractor. We do not embed third-party advertising retargeting pixels (such as Meta/Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or Google Display Remarketing), cross-site tracking scripts, or data broker tags on our website.

While we use Google Analytics and Google Search Console to monitor page performance, search rankings, and user flows, your browsing activity is never packaged, rented, or sold to external programmatic ad networks.

6. Browser Management & Opt-Out Options

You can manage, restrict, or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings menu:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari (macOS & iOS): Preferences / Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Opting Out of Google Analytics: In addition to browser cookie controls, you may prevent Google Analytics from collecting visit telemetry by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Operational Note: Disabling all cookies in your browser will not affect your ability to browse public service pages or view price packages. However, you will be unable to log into the PWS Client Portal, as secure session tokens are required to verify account identity.

7. Global Privacy Control (GPC) & Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Our web servers respect automated browser privacy signals. When our systems detect a valid Global Privacy Control (GPC) or "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal transmitted by your browser, we automatically suppress non-essential analytics tracking in compliance with California privacy standards.
