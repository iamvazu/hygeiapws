Cookie Policy

Learn how and why we utilize cookies and local storage on our website.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Status: Active Entity: Hygeia Power Wash Solutions

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit websites. They help websites remember preferences, calculate interactive pricing, and enhance page load performance.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Operational Cookies: Required for fundamental site operations, such as keeping track of your selected subscription frequency ($35/month vs $65 one-time) and processing quote forms.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your cookie consent choices (`hygeia_cookie_consent`) so you are not repeatedly prompted on future visits.
  • Performance & Analytics: Help us measure anonymous visitor metrics, load times, and broken links so we can continuously improve Peninsula service delivery.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust your cookie settings through our on-site banner or by configuring your web browser settings to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact interactive pricing calculators and instant booking widgets.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our cookie practices, email us at aloha@hygeiaservices.com.
