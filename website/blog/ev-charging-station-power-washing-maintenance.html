Commercial EV Charging Station Power Washing & Pad Maintenance for Silicon Valley Tech Campuses

Published on 2026-04-05 by Hygeia Commercial Operations

Category: Tech Campuses Region: Silicon Valley & Peninsula, CA Author: Hygeia Commercial Operations

With hundreds of electric vehicle (EV) charging stations installed across corporate campuses in Cupertino, Menlo Park, and Palo Alto, EV parking stalls have become a central hub of daily employee foot traffic. While EVs don't leak engine oil, conventional vehicles frequently park in these stalls, leaving dark oil spots, coolant drips, and coffee spills right where users handle charging cables.

Safety Precautions Around High-Voltage Charging Equipment

Power washing around Level 2 and DC Fast Chargers requires specialized electrical safety training. High-pressure streams must never be directed into cable holsters, vents, or electrical junction cabinets.

Hygeia EV Station Maintenance Protocol

  • Pedestal & Holster Soft Cleaning: Hand-detailing charging screens and cable handles with non-conductive, antimicrobial sanitizers.
  • Concrete Pad Degreasing: Hot-water rotary surface scrubbing that restores clean white and green EV stall striping.
  • Debris & Leaf Extraction: Clearing drainage grates around charging banks to prevent water ponding around high-voltage hardware during winter rains.
