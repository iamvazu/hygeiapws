California Privacy Rights

Disclosures under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Statute: Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq. (CCPA/CPRA) Jurisdiction: California
Notice at Collection for California Residents: This section supplements our general Privacy Policy and applies exclusively to California residents. We collect identifiers and service addresses solely for fulfilling power washing and trash bin cleaning services. We do NOT sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

1. Categories of Personal Information Collected

In the preceding 12 months, Hygeia Power Wash Solutions has collected the following categories of California consumer data:

  • Category A — Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, and service property address.
  • Category B — Customer Records: Property specifications, service notes, and payment confirmation tokens.
  • Category F — Internet Activity: Website interaction and page visit telemetry.
  • Category I — Visual Information: Photos of exterior surfaces uploaded voluntarily by clients for quote generation.

2. California Consumer Rights

Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents possess specific legal rights regarding their personal information:

  1. Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request what specific personal information we have collected, used, and disclosed about you over the past 12 months.
  2. Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal record-keeping exceptions.
  3. Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  4. Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: Hygeia does NOT sell or share personal information for financial consideration or behavioral tracking.
  5. Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny service, charge different rates, or provide inferior quality for exercising your California privacy rights.

3. How to Submit a Verified CCPA Request

California residents or their authorized agents may submit a request by:

  • Email: aloha@hygeiaservices.com with the subject line "CCPA Consumer Request"
  • Telephone: (650) 933-3823 (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM PT)
  • Mail: Hygeia Power Wash Solutions, Attn: Legal Compliance, 1105 Benton St, Santa Clara, CA 95050

We verify requests by matching identifying details (such as your name, service address, and email) with records in our secure database. We respond to verified requests within 45 days as required by California law.
