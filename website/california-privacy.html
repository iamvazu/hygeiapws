7. Your Statutory California Consumer Rights

California residents hold the following rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • 1. Right to Know & Access: You may request the categories of personal information we have collected, the categories of sources, the business purposes, the categories of third parties to whom data was disclosed, and the specific pieces of personal information collected about you in the preceding 12 months.
  • 2. Right to Delete: You may request that we delete personal information collected from you, subject to legal and statutory accounting retention exceptions.
  • 3. Right to Correct Inaccurate Personal Information: You may request immediate correction of inaccurate or outdated information in your customer record.
  • 4. Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny service, charge different rates, or provide a lower quality of wash service because you exercised your CCPA/CPRA rights.

8. Submitting a Verifiable Request

To exercise your California Right to Know, Access, Correct, or Delete, please submit a verifiable request through one of our designated channels:

  • Direct Telephone: Call our dispatch headquarters at (650) 933-3823 and state that you are making a "California CCPA Consumer Request."
  • Direct Email: Email aloha@hygeiaservices.com with the subject line "CCPA / CPRA Request — Hygeia PWS".
  • Postal Mail: Montero Enterprises Inc., Attn: California Privacy Officer, 1105 Benton St, Suite A, Santa Clara, CA 95050.

9. Identity Verification Standards

To prevent unauthorized disclosure of customer addresses or service details, we must verify your identity before fulfilling any request:

  • Two-Point Verification: Matching at least two identifying data points provided in your request (such as full name, email address, and service street address) against verified records in our dispatch database.
  • Three-Point Verification: For requests seeking specific pieces of personal information, matching at least three verified data points plus a signed declaration under penalty of perjury.
  • We confirm receipt within ten (10) business days and provide substantive response within forty-five (45) calendar days.

10. Authorized Agent Representation

You may designate an authorized legal agent to submit a request on your behalf. Authorized agents must provide written, signed authorization from the consumer and verify their own identity, or present proof of valid power of attorney under California Probate Code §§ 4121 to 4130.

11. California "Shine the Light" Law Disclosures

Under California Civil Code § 1798.83 ("Shine the Light"), California residents may request information regarding disclosure of personal information to third parties for direct marketing. Hygeia does not disclose personal information to third parties for direct marketing purposes.
