How It Works

Curbside trash & recycling bin sanitization made effortless for Silicon Valley homeowners.

1

1. Curbside

We begin our sanitizing service immediately after your local trash collection. At your scheduled time and location, we arrive promptly to clean your bins right where they stand. This simple process ensures a thorough and efficient cleaning experience.

2

2. Clean

Experience complete cleanliness in just 30 seconds. Our advanced 360-degree cleaning heads reach every nook and cranny of your trash bin, using pressurized water heated to over 200°.

3

3. Disinfect

We don't just clean—we sanitize, transforming your dirty trash bin into a spotless, hygienic container. Our powerful system combines hot water with an eco-friendly disinfectant to eliminate 99% of germs and bacteria.

4

4. Done

After completing our thorough cleaning process, we return your garbage can to your preferred location. You can rest easy knowing your bins are clean, disinfected, deodorized, and back where they belong.
